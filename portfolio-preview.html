// about me

Nice to meet you.

Hi! I'm Rahma Khoyrul Hawa, a 12th-grade vocational high school student majoring in Software and Game Development (RPL) at SMK Negeri 2 Cimahi. I'm passionate about programming and currently focusing on improving my skills in web development by exploring new technologies and building projects that challenge me to keep learning.

Outside of programming, I enjoy expressing my creativity through drawing, crocheting, and making various handmade crafts. I also run a small crochet brand called Freaknit, where I design and create handmade products. Through this journey, I've learned not only crafting skills but also creativity, consistency, branding, and how to turn ideas into something meaningful.

I believe that technology and creativity go hand in hand. Whether I'm developing a website or creating something by hand, I always strive to build experiences that are functional, thoughtful, and visually appealing. I'm excited to continue growing as a developer and creator while contributing to meaningful projects in the future.
